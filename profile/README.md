# SKAWINSKI GmbH

## 🚀 Who We Are

We are an Austrian IT company specializing in **IT automation, DevOps, and cloud migration**. Since 2022, we've been helping businesses across the DACH region optimize their IT infrastructure through intelligent automation, delivering more productive time.

*Yes, we're from Austria - Mozart, not kangaroos.* 🎼

## 💡 Our Mission
